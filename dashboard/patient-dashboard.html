Frequently Asked Questions

You can book an appointment by clicking on "Find Doctors" in the sidebar, selecting your preferred doctor, and choosing an available time slot. You can also book directly from the dashboard by clicking "Book Appointment" in the Quick Actions section.

Navigate to "Medical Records" in the sidebar. Here you can view, download, and share all your medical documents including lab reports, imaging results, and clinical notes.

Go to "Prescriptions" in the sidebar, find the medication you need to refill, and click the "Refill" button. Your doctor will review and approve the request within 24-48 hours.

We accept credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. You can also pay using your Health Savings Account (HSA) or Flexible Spending Account (FSA) cards.
